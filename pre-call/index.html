Preview Property, company setups, cars or software

Property, company setups, cars or software

Hey {FIRST NAME},

The question my team hears most before a call is: "Will this work for what I sell?"

Here's the honest answer.

The three moves work for anything expensive. A buyer of an apartment and a buyer of a company setup both decide in the first minute whether to listen. Both say "it's too expensive." Both leave you on read after you send the details.

What changes is the words. That's why in week one my team writes your opening, your two questions and your follow-up messages for your product.

On the call, tell us exactly what you sell and who buys it. We'll show you what your version sounds like.

Mansi

Preview You only need to know what you'll ask next

You only need to know what you'll ask next

Hey {FIRST NAME},

Many people tell my team, "I'm just not a confident person."

I wasn't either. In 2019 I rushed every call just to get it over with.

Confidence on a call comes from one thing: knowing what you'll ask next. When you know that, you stop talking too much, you stop giving discounts and you stop sounding nervous.

That can be learned. It's what we practise on your recorded calls every week.

If this is on your mind, say it on the call. It's the most common thing we fix.

Mansi
