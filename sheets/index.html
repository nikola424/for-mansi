Free sheet

The "send me the details" script

Use this the next time a client says: "I'm busy. Just send me the details."

Say I don't want to send you a random package that doesn't fit what you need, and I'm guessing you don't want that either. Why don't we do a quick call tomorrow, around 10 or 11, whatever works, and I'll tell you exactly what makes sense for you? Then I'll send you the details.

Why it works If you send the PDF, you become one of ten. When you look like the other nine, the only thing left to compare is the price.

Remember If they don't give you 10 minutes, they were never going to give you money.

Free sheet

3 hello scripts

Open the call with curiosity, so the client listens.

Website lead Hi Mike, it's Mansi. I see you dropped something on our website this morning. I wanted to understand what you were comparing us against, so I can help you better.

Viewing request Hi Mike, it's Mansi. I saw you were looking at the apartment on the marina. What made you pick that one out of everything you saw?

Old enquiry Hi Mike, it's Mansi. You asked us about a company setup a while ago. I'm curious what you ended up doing.

Remember Same call, same reason for calling. Only the words and the tone change.

Free sheet

The 2 questions

Use these when a client says it's too expensive.

Question 1 "Compared to what?" Then stop talking. Let it get awkward.

Question 2 "Makes sense. Can I ask you something? What are they leaving out of that price? Because a lot of companies take a few things out so the number looks lower."

Then say I don't do that, because I want a real relationship with you, and I'd like referrals from you later.

Remember You never defend your price. You ask two questions, and they do the maths themselves.

4 dead-lead messages

Send them in this order.

1. Ask what went wrong Hey Ahmed, this isn't a pitch. I'm just curious what made you go the other way. It helps me get better.

2. Check in Hey, hope everything's going smoothly with your setup. How are you finding it? If you need anything, I'm just a text away.

3. Bring something new We have a faster option now, and I thought of you straight away.

4. The one-line text Hey, are you still looking to [what they wanted], or should I close your file? Either way, please let me know.

Remember A no isn't forever. It's just a no for now.

Free sheet

The 5-word cheat sheet

Read your last call back. Find the words you used.

"Just" Say: "Mike, following up on what we discussed yesterday."

"Honestly" If it's true, you don't need to announce it.

"Try" Say: "Mike, it'll be done by Sunday." If you can't promise it, give a date you can.

"Cheap" or "affordable" Say: "It's an investment of [your price]."

"Does that make sense?" Say: "What questions do you have so far?"

Free sheet

The 4 rules for rich clients

Read these before a big meeting.

1 Rich clients don't want more. They want less friction.

2 Don't describe your product. Describe life after it.

3 Premium buyers can smell desperation. Don't over-explain. Don't discount before they ask.

4 Make the buying experience match the price. The sale starts before they pay.
